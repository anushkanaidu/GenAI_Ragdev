# Onboarding Stages

Onboarding has eight stages:

1. Intake: Sign your offer, complete background verification, and submit HR paperwork before Day 1.
2. IT Setup: Receive your laptop and get access to all core tools on Day 1.
3. Orientation: Attend the company orientation session during your first week.
4. Meet Your Team: Have introductory meetings with your teammates in your first week.
5. Training: Complete all mandatory training within your first 14 days.
6. First Project: Receive your first project from your manager by the end of week two.
7. 30-Day Check-in: Review your progress and goals with your manager after 30 days.
8. 90-Day Review: A formal review of your onboarding with your manager and HR at 90 days.

Your onboarding buddy is available throughout all stages to answer questions.
