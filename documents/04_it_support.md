# IT Support

## How to get help
For any hardware, software, or access issue, email the IT team at it-support@company.com. The IT team responds within one business day. For urgent issues that stop you from working, post in the #it-help Slack channel as well.

## Your laptop
Your company laptop is shipped to your home address before Day 1. If it has not arrived by your start date, email IT Support.

## Passwords and accounts
Password resets are done through the self-service password portal. If you are locked out of your account, contact IT Support.

## Lost or stolen devices
Report a lost or stolen company device to IT Support within 24 hours so the device can be locked remotely.
