# Employee Benefits

## Health insurance
Medical, dental, and vision coverage starts on the first day of the month after your start date. For example, if you start on March 10, your coverage begins on April 1.

## Enrollment
You must enroll in benefits within 30 days of your start date through the HR Portal. If you miss this window, you can only enroll during the next open enrollment period or after a qualifying life event.

## Retirement
The company matches 401(k) contributions up to 4% of your salary. You can enroll at any time.

## Learning budget
Each employee receives a $1,000 learning budget per year for courses, books, and conferences.
