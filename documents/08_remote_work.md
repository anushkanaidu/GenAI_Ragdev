# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## Hybrid schedule
The company follows a hybrid model. Employees are expected to work from the office three days per week, on Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday. Monday and Friday are flexible remote days.

## Fully remote roles
Some roles are approved as fully remote. Your offer letter states whether your role is hybrid or fully remote.

## Home office stipend
Every employee receives a one-time home office stipend of $500 for equipment such as a monitor, chair, or desk. Claim it through the expense reimbursement process within your first 60 days.

## Working from another location
Working from a different city for more than two weeks requires manager approval. Working from another country requires approval from HR.
