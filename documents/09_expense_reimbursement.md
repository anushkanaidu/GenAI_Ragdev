# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Submitting expenses
Submit work-related expenses through the HR Portal within 30 days of the purchase date. Expenses submitted after 30 days may not be reimbursed.

## Receipts
A receipt is required for any expense over $25.

## Approval and payment
Your manager must approve each expense report. Approved expenses are paid in the next payroll cycle.

## Travel
For approved business travel, meals are reimbursed up to $50 per day. Book flights and hotels through the company travel portal.
