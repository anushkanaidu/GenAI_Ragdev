# Background Verification (BGV) Policy

## When it happens
Background verification must be completed before your first day (Day 1). Your offer of employment is contingent on a successful background verification.

## Vendors by country
- Employees based in the United States complete background verification through HireRight.
- Employees based in the United Kingdom complete background verification through Credence.

## What to provide
You will receive an email from the vendor asking for a government-issued photo ID, your education history, and your employment history for the past five years. Please respond within 3 business days of receiving the email.

## Timeline
Verification usually takes 5 to 7 business days. If it is not complete by your scheduled start date, HR may move your start date. Questions about the process go to the HR team, not the vendor.
