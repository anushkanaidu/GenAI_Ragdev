# Tools and Software

## Core tools
Every employee uses the following tools:
- Slack for team chat and announcements. New hires should join the #general and #new-hires channels.
- Asana for project and task tracking.
- Figma for design files and reviews.
- Gemini as the approved AI assistant for drafting and research.
- Google Workspace (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs) for email, meetings, and documents.

## Getting access
Accounts for all core tools are created during the IT Setup stage of onboarding. You will receive login instructions at your company email address on Day 1.

## Approved use
Only company-approved tools may be used for company data. Do not paste confidential information into AI tools other than Gemini. Requests for additional software go through IT Support.
