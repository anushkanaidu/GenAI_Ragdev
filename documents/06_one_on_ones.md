# 1-on-1 Meetings

## Schedule
You meet with your manager in a 1-on-1 meeting every week on Friday. Each meeting lasts 30 minutes. Your manager sends a recurring calendar invite during your first week.

## Agenda
Keep a shared 1-on-1 document with your manager. Add topics before each meeting, such as progress on your work, blockers, questions, and feedback.

## Rescheduling
If you or your manager cannot attend, reschedule the meeting within the same week rather than skipping it.

## Skip-level meetings
Once a month, you also have a 30-minute skip-level meeting with your manager's manager.
