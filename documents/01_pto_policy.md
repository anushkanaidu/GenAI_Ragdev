# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

## Annual allowance
All full-time employees receive 20 days of Paid Time Off (PTO) per calendar year. PTO accrues monthly at 1.67 days per month, starting on your first day of employment. Part-time employees receive PTO on a prorated basis.

## Requesting time off
All PTO requests must be submitted through the HR Portal. Requests of three or more consecutive days should be submitted at least two weeks in advance. Your manager approves or declines requests within three business days.

## Carryover
Up to 5 unused PTO days may be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by March 31; any carried-over days not used by then are forfeited.

## Sick leave
Sick leave is separate from PTO. Employees receive 10 sick days per year, which do not carry over. Sick leave of more than three consecutive days requires a note to HR.
