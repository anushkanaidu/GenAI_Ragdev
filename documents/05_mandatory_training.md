# Mandatory Training

## Required courses
All new employees must complete three mandatory training courses:
1. Harassment Prevention Policy
2. AI Ethics
3. Tool Onboarding

## Deadline
All three courses must be completed within your first 14 days of employment.

## Where to take them
Courses are available on the Learning Portal, which you can open from the HR Portal. Each course takes between 30 and 60 minutes and ends with a short quiz. You need a score of 80% or higher to pass.

## Missed deadlines
If a course is not completed within 14 days, your manager and HR are notified automatically. Incomplete mandatory training must be finished before your 30-Day Check-in.
